| 2026-10-24 | v0.5 | **Speed detection works from a recording of the TXD-in pin** (RMT receiver, 1 us steps, burst ends after 32 ms idle, ping-pong so a full 128-character line fits). In command mode the typing is decoded in software from that recording and the UART's copy is thrown away; online, or with a fixed `AT*BAUD`, the UART is used (with flow control). Rates: 300, 600, 1200, 2400, 4800, 9600, 19200, 38400, 57600, 115200 (bit time within 6 %). A new speed is taken only from "AT", "at" or a lone "A"/"a". Command-mode typing reaches the parser about 35 ms late (not noticeable) | The Arduino core's detection can't see rates below 9600 on the S3. Decoding the recording means the first "AT" at a new speed is understood, with no garbled echo | Proposed by Claude, checked on the bench |
