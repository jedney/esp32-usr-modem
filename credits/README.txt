RetroExternalModem by luizpacheco on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7063155

Summary:
It was designed to accommodate a Raspberry Pi 3 B+ and some other components.Tried to make it almost identical to the iconic external USRobotcs Sportster modem.You can print it and follow this project https://github.com/caiot5/retroexternalmodem to get a fully operational WIFI modem acting as serial for your retro pcs.There are some arts to print (plain paper or stick paper) to make ir realistic.
